## 3 lines on why it's hard. 

Reasons why it's difficult:
1. Multiple LLM agents have to collaborate via state, and any flaw in one small element (flawed plan or faulty tool call) is compounded in such way that the workflow should be controlled in terms of graph, with retries and human approvals instead of loose chaining.

2. Research requires factual answers. Finding a way to combine FAISS/ChromaDB retrieval, chunking and source tracking for claim-evidence tracking is tricky, and errors (hallucinations, unrelated retrievals) do not produce obvious feedback.

3. LLM is non-deterministic and thus requires tracing and retestable evaluations, instead of simple unit tests.
